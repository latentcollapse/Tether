# Tether 2.1.13 — small, explicit command surface

- Makes the daily CLI contract obvious: `send`, `resolve`, `inbox`, and `delivery`.
- Makes the optional dashboard explicit with `tether dashboard`; bare `tether` now prints help.
- Keeps compatibility, operator, board, and diagnostic commands working without presenting them as equal parts of the core workflow.
- Adds `tether --help-all` for intentional discovery of those advanced commands.
- Removes argparse's leaked `==SUPPRESS==` placeholder from normal help.
